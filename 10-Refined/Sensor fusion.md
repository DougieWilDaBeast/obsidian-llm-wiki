# Sensor fusion

> [!review]
> Stub created from a single passing mention. Promote and flesh out if it recurs.

Combining readings from multiple sensors (and over time) into a more accurate or richer
picture than any single sensor gives. Relevant to the [[Backyard Weather Station]] as a
*later* goal — e.g. cross-checking the [[BME280]] against a second temperature source, or
fusing pressure trends to predict weather.
