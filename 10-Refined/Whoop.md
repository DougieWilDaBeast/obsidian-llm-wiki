# Whoop

A strap-style wearable that scores daily recovery, sleep and strain. Its data reaches the vault
through `pipeline/wearables/` (one immutable JSON per day in `00-Raw/Wearables/whoop/`) and is
summarised in weekly digests, never one page per day (CLAUDE.md §8b).
